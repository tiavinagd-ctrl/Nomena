# DIGITALSUBMG — PREMIUM CINEMATIC 3D BRAND COMMERCIAL

**Production type:** High-end 3D motion design / Luxury SaaS commercial

**Duration:** Exactly 50 seconds

**Brand:** DigitalSubMG

**Official website:** https://www.digitalsub.org/

**Campaign tagline:** « Vos abonnements premium en un clic. »

**Final delivery:** 4K UHD, 16:9, 24 FPS

## 1. CREATIVE VISION

Create a spectacular, sophisticated, visually addictive 50-second 3D commercial for DigitalSubMG, a digital subscription platform based in Madagascar.

The advertisement must feel like a world-class product launch film produced by an elite international motion design studio.

Visual inspiration: premium Apple-style product cinematography, Stripe-inspired SaaS animations, sophisticated 3D interface visualization, luxury technology advertising, and contemporary glassmorphism.

The result must feel premium, credible, futuristic, elegant, and commercially persuasive.

**Core message:** DigitalSubMG simplifies access to premium digital subscriptions, with convenient local payment in Malagasy Ariary.

Build a continuous visual story following this sequence:

**DISCOVER → EXPLORE → CHOOSE → PAY → ACTIVATE → GET SUPPORT → DIGITALSUBMG**

Every scene must flow naturally into the next through deliberate, elegant visual transitions.

## 2. MASTER VISUAL IDENTITY

### Color and lighting

Use a sophisticated palette inspired by the supplied official DigitalSubMG branding:

- Rich royal-purple gradients and luminous violet highlights
- Soft lavender backgrounds
- Premium white and pearl-white surfaces
- Subtle transparent glass materials
- Controlled chrome and metallic accents
- Soft contact shadows and realistic reflections

Purple is the signature color throughout the film.

Avoid excessive neon, oversaturation, dark gaming aesthetics, or childish cartoon environments.

### Rendering

Ultra-clean photorealistic 3D motion design with physically based rendering, ray-traced reflections, ambient occlusion, realistic refraction, premium material shaders, soft global illumination, cinematic depth of field, and beautiful studio lighting.

Use balanced compositions, satisfying negative space, subtle atmospheric particles, and carefully designed visual hierarchy.

### Camera direction

Use sophisticated commercial cinematography:

- Slow controlled dolly-in movements
- Elegant orbital camera movements
- Smooth cinematic push-ins
- Precise macro close-ups
- Subtle parallax depth
- Natural easing and fluid camera acceleration
- Seamless match cuts and object-morphing transitions

No shaky cameras, chaotic zooms, abrupt cuts, or unnecessary camera rotations.

### Brand consistency lock

Use one consistent flagship smartphone design throughout the advertisement: a modern premium device with a graphite metallic frame, edge-to-edge glass display, rounded corners, and realistic screen reflections.

Use the official DigitalSubMG logo supplied as a reference asset. Never recreate it with inaccurate typography.

Keep all recurring objects, interface elements, purple gradients, materials, lighting direction, and visual proportions consistent between scenes.

If a reference image is provided, treat it as the definitive visual source.

## 4. MOTION DESIGN LANGUAGE

All motion should be intentionally choreographed.

Use refined cinematic timing, seamless object transformations, elegant soft-body-inspired easing where appropriate, and polished spatial transitions.

Object movements should feel physically believable even within a stylized, futuristic environment.

Avoid random floating motion, excessive bouncing, chaotic particles, or unrelated visual effects.

Use recurring purple luminous energy as a connecting visual motif throughout the advertisement.

The entire 50-second film must feel like one unified commercial rather than seven independent AI-generated clips.

- Light cinematic bass
- Subtle interface clicks
- Soft atmospheric whooshes
- Refined digital notification tones
- One recognizable brand-identifying sonic signature

## 6. TYPOGRAPHY AND LOGO ACCURACY

Use a sophisticated contemporary sans-serif typography system.

Keep on-screen copy minimal, correctly accented in French, and visually aligned with the official brand identity.

No more than one main message per scene.

Maintain generous margins and clear readability on mobile and desktop screens.

Use original high-resolution brand assets whenever available.

If the generation model cannot reproduce typography and third-party service logos reliably, generate clean animation plates with reserved space, then composite the exact text, official logo files, and service logos during post-production.

Never generate visually distorted or misspelled brand names.

Do not suggest official commercial partnerships or endorsements by the third-party services.
